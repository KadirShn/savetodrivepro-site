Save to Drive Pro
for Google Drive™ · Google Drive™ için

Save web pages, images and files to your Google Drive™

A Chrome extension that saves the page you are on as a real PDF, a visible-area or full-page PNG, an MHTML web archive or a Google Doc – into the Drive folder you choose. Right-click to save images and linked files.

Coming soon to the Chrome Web Store.

Private
